The Art of Asking

Prompt Like You S.E.R.V.E.

A prompt is simply what you type or say to AI. Include these five pieces and you’ll get better results every time. Tap the microphone if you’d rather talk than type.

S
SituationWho you are and what’s going on“I’m the church clerk at a 120-member congregation.”
E
ExpectationExactly what you need it to make“Write a one-page welcome letter…”
R
RecipientWho will read it or see it“…for first-time visitors, many with young children.”
V
VoiceHow it should sound“Make it warm and joyful, not preachy.”
E
ExtrasLength, format and must-have details“Keep it under 200 words and include our Sunday 11 AM service.”
WEAK PROMPT

“Make a flyer for our church event.”

S.E.R.V.E. PROMPT

“I’m the media coordinator at a Pentecostal church. Create a flyer for our Family & Friends Day on Sunday at 11 AM. It’s for neighbors who have never visited. Make it warm, joyful and welcoming. Use royal purple and gold, mention the cookout after service, and keep the words short and easy to read.”

Four Pro Tips

Add your words afterward

AI often misspells words inside pictures. Add titles, dates and times in Canva, Word or PowerPoint.

Ask for room for a title

Say “leave empty space at the top” so your words have a home.

Change one thing at a time

Not quite right? Say “make it brighter” or “add more people” and try again.

Honor real people

Never make fake photos of real members, pastors or bishops. Use real photos for real people.

Free tools to try: Microsoft Designer / Copilot, Canva (Magic Media), ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Adobe Express. Most have free versions with daily limits.

Safety First

Guard the Gate: Five Rules

Keep private things private

No names with prayer requests, giving records, phone numbers or health details.

Check every Scripture

AI can misquote verses or invent references. Open your Bible.

Verify facts, dates and names

It can sound very confident and still be wrong.

Respect copyright

Show song lyrics only under your church’s license (like CCLI). Don’t copy others’ art.

You have the final say

AI drafts. People decide. The Holy Spirit leads.
