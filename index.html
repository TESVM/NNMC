Our Mission

About This Council

The Northern North Ministerial Council exists to strengthen ministers, foster unity, and create room for prayerful leadership development across the region.

This gathering brings pastors, elders, ministry leaders, and workers together for fellowship, preaching, practical instruction, and shared encouragement.
